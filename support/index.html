Questions

Where do I start?

TRAINING on the main menu, lessons 1 to 5, in order. The campaign's first serial assumes all five.

The text is small on my phone.

SETTINGS → LARGE TEXT. It is on by default on iPhone, and changes the track table and the lesson narration to the larger font. iPad is the comfortable device.

The opening screen flickers.

SETTINGS → CRT GLOW → OFF removes the flicker and the phosphor glow. The game also honours your device's Reduce Motion setting, which turns both off regardless.

I got a phone call mid-serial.

The game pauses and the pause screen comes up; RESUME continues. A serial in progress is also kept for CONTINUE on the main menu when the app is left.

What does EMCON / ROE / ASH / TEWA mean?

REFERENCE → GLOSSARY, and lesson 2 of the tutorial.

Why did I lose with the ship undamaged?

A serial is not won on kills. Look at the debrief's objective list: a failed REQ line is why. Harming a neutral or friendly unit fails the rules-of-engagement objective on every serial.

Is this a real navy's system?

No. The console is fictional (CMS 41A), inspired by the vocabulary of real combat-management systems. The ship, HMCS Burrard (FFH 342), is fictional: Halifax-class in her fit, but no commissioned RCN ship has carried the name and the class does not use the pennant. No real system, classified material or photograph of a real console was used.

Restore purchases / accounts.

There are none. The game is a single purchase with no in-app purchases.
