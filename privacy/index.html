Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Assumed Hostile is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It makes no network connections of any kind.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, tutorial and campaign progress, career totals and a saved serial in progress are stored locally in the app's own container, so that they are there the next time you open it. This data never leaves your device unless you back it up yourself through iCloud or iTunes device backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects or transmits anything, this policy will say so before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated with it.
