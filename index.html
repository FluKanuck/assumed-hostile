Assumed Hostile

A tactical naval command-and-control game for iPhone and iPad.

You are the watchkeeper. Everything on the screen is unknown until you say otherwise — and the rules of engagement apply to what you declare, not to what is true.

Build the picture from radar, passive electronic support and IFF. Decide what each contact is before you decide what to do about it. Then fight the ship.

The game runs entirely on your device. It is offline: no account, no ads, no purchases, and nothing collected.
